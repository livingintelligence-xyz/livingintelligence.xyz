About Living Intelligence

Intelligence for living, that lives.

Living Intelligence is an independent technology studio in San Francisco, founded by engineer and classical musician Álex Filipe Santos. We build software and hardware that carry everyday complexity, giving people more freedom to live, work, and create.

Our legal name is Living Intelligence LLC, and our official website is livingintelligence.xyz.

A human starting point

Our work grows from problems close to home: keeping files together across services, making connected devices work together, and finding simpler ways to interact with the spaces we inhabit.

Behind these problems is a larger question: what should technology take care of, and what should remain ours to decide?

We believe people should be able to delegate routine tasks while keeping authority over the choices that matter. An intelligent system should make its actions understandable, welcome correction, and leave you free to change your mind. Trust grows when you can let something work and still know how to step in.

Independence, built in

Your choice of a device or service should leave room for other choices. We build toward systems that work across providers, keep data under your control, and make the things you already own more useful together.

That belief shapes our commitment to local-first design, privacy, and open-source foundations. It also shapes how we think about longevity: useful technology deserves to keep working as the world around it changes.

What we’re building

Aetherloom brings this philosophy to your files. A native macOS app in development with an open-source synchronization engine, it is designed to keep folders aligned across cloud services, local storage, and network drives. The aim is simple: use the services that suit you, with readable copies of your data in places you control.

Home Intelligence brings it into the home. We’re developing local-first home automation, room-centered interfaces, and custom hardware that bring lighting, sound, and connected devices into a coherent experience. Built on Home Assistant, the work starts with the layout and life of each home, making everyday control approachable to the people who share it.

These are the first expressions of a broader direction: intelligence that can take initiative, remain accountable, and grow with the people it serves. We measure progress by what becomes easier, what people gain the freedom to do, and how well a system earns its place in everyday life.
